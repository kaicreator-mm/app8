# Product Research Backlog — app8

Status: NON_BLOCKING_ROADMAP_RESEARCH

This file contains empirical/product questions that are neither current L1 Product Freeze blockers nor L2 architecture authority.

## R-001 — Hard-case adaptation distribution

Question:

> How often do valuable non-trivial software Providers require deeper-than-thin adaptation, and what adaptation levels remain economically acceptable?

Why this is not L2_REQUIRED:

- it asks about the population/economics of future Provider coverage, not how the frozen v0.1 architecture should be constructed;
- the Provider-native v0.1 thesis does not require universal or automatic adaptation.

Future evidence should sample stateful/headless/desktop-core and other harder software rather than only mature CLI-heavy tools.

A future product version that claims scalable automatic adaptation must promote this question into explicit Product Evidence with its own protocol and gate.
