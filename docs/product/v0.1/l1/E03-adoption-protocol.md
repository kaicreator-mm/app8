# E03 — Adoption Evidence Protocol

Status: PREREGISTRATION_DRAFT  
Gate: L1-ADOPT  
Supports: PCL-005

## Hypothesis

Target users have a recurring current problem around software/tool behavior verification, admission or upgrade currentness and will commit concrete pilot resources.

## Cohort

Freeze exactly 8 external participants before interviewing.

Minimum composition:

- >= 3 Agent/tool-platform practitioners;
- >= 3 security/governance practitioners;
- remaining participants may be tool/MCP publishers or either primary segment.

Participants must be outside the app8 authoring organization/team.

Recruitment channel and inclusion criteria are preregistered.

## Interview success record

A participant counts as positive only if both are present:

1. a concrete recurring current problem in:
   - tool admission;
   - behavior verification;
   - or upgrade/currentness;
2. a concrete pilot commitment.

Pilot commitment means at least one:

- named pilot and scheduled evaluation window;
- representative Provider/tool dataset;
- access to a representative test environment;
- committed engineering/security review time.

General interest, praise or hypothetical willingness does not count.

## PASS

~~~text
>= 5 / 8 positive participants
~~~

and positives include at least two stakeholder classes.

## FAIL

Fewer than 5/8 positives after the frozen cohort is complete.

A new cohort cannot erase this result unless a successor Product Evidence version changes the target segment and receives Independent Review.

## What Was NOT Proven

E03 does not prove:

- willingness to pay at a specific price;
- market size;
- architecture feasibility;
- technical conformance quality.
